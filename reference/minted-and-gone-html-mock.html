How to read this guide

Minted & Gone records NFT marketplaces as historical entries, not as investment recommendations. Each record asks whether the marketplace is active, limited, inactive, dead, acquired, merged, rebranded, or unknown.

What counts

An entry should have a distinct marketplace identity, a URL or archive trail, and meaningful marketplace behavior such as listing, buying, bidding, secondary trading, or marketplace-style mint distribution.

Status definitions

Active

Core marketplace functions are represented as available.

Limited

Only some functions remain, such as browsing, withdrawal, or migration.

Dead

The marketplace operation is represented as ended, even if on-chain assets may remain.

Unknown

Evidence is insufficient. The site should avoid pretending certainty.

Evidence and confidence

Official statements, support pages, company announcements, archived pages, and credible reporting should be preferred. Community references can help discovery, but should not alone prove shutdown or asset loss.

Stats note

Stats describe this registry’s current coverage and quality, not the complete global NFT marketplace universe.
